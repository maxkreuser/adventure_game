# Adventure Game - Plan for game steps

## Description

I will write the story for our game and choose the plan the different decision
steps for our game. Different decisions will bring to different paths, a
path will converge back with another. Some decisions will bring the user to a
dead-end or to a morbid death.

### Game Steps and Paths

### 1. Start

1. You fell asleep studying. You wake up. No one is in the library.

2. It's night, time to leave. Do you take the stairwell or the elevator?

### 1.1 Stairwell Path

1. The stairwell has no light except for a small emergency blinking red light.

2. You exit into the atrium. No one is there, not even the guards.

3. You notice two dogs by the front door.

4. Do you walk by the dogs or turn around to exit by the back?

### 1.1.1 Stairwell - Dog Path

1. The dogs are salivating and their eyes are red.

2. They both run and jumps to bite your face. DEAD

### 1.1.2 Stairwell - Back Exit Path

1. Before reaching the back door, a guard turns around the corner.

2. He is walking slowly, you notice he has an open wound on his cheek.

3. Do you ask him if he needs help or walk by quickly at a distance?

### 1.1.2.1 Stairwell - Back Exit - Help Guard Path

1. You ask the guard if he's ok.

2. The guard grabs you in his arms, you struggle, he bites your neck and you vision goes red. DEAD

### 1.1.2.2 Stairwell - Back Exit - Avoid Guard Path

1. You walk by the guard and exit by the back door.

2. You are relieved, there is a big group of people outside huddled together.

3. You walk towards them. They all turn their heads at the same time to look at you.

4. Their faces are all demented and injured. They all start running towards you.

5. Do you fight them or run away?

### 1.1.2.2.1 Stairwell - Back Exit - Avoid Guard - Fight Path

1. You lift your two fists in front of you.

2. They jump all over you, everything turns black. DEAD

### 1.1.2.2.2 Stairwell - Back Exit - Avoid Guard - Run Path

1. You see your friend inside his car. You start running towards it.

2. You feel your shirt pulled from behind. Many hands are grabbing your limbs. DEAD

### 1.2 Elevator Path

1. The elevator is moving down. A big loud mechanical noise happens.

2. The elevator stops abruptly. A few seconds pass. The elevator start shaking.

3. Do you press the help button or try to pull the doors open?

### 1.2.1 Elevator - Button Path

1. A groaning, gurgling voice answers with screeching noise over it.

2. You hear a loud snap. The elevator starts free falling. DEAD

### 1.2.2 Elevator - Door Path

1. To your surprise the doors open. You exit on the second floor.

2. The hallway lights are blinking, everything is quiet.

3. You walk towards the skybridge to reach the neighboring building.

4. You pass a stairwell. Do you take it or keep walking to the skybridge?

### 1.2.2.1 Elevator - Door - Stair Path

1. GOES TO - ### 1.1 Stairwell Path - 2.

### 1.2.2.2 Elevator - Door - Skybridge Path

1. On the skybridge, you feel a wind current and see paper sheets floating in the air.

2. A window is broken. You look outside and see a group of people.

3. They are leaning over an unconscious person and bitting his body.

4. Panic! You run to the new building. There you see your favorite teacher.

5. He asks if you need help. Do you say 'yes' or 'no'?

### 1.2.2.2.1 Elevator - Door - Yes Path

1. He says not to worry. You calm down.

2. You feel a painful pinch in your neck. You see a syringe and your teacher smiling. Your vision goes red. DEAD

### 1.2.2.2.2 Elevator - Door - No Path

1. You run towards the large staircase and hear a loud growling.

2. Going down the staircase, a large creature appears at the bottom.

3. It is third pig, third wolf, third man wearing a torn up guard uniform.

4. Do you jump down from the side or use your phone?

### 1.2.2.2.2.1 Elevator - Door - No - Jump Path

1. As you land you break your ankle and can't get up.

2. The creature appears above you. It opens its mouth. Green red blood drips onto your face. DEAD

### 1.2.2.2.2.2 Elevator - Door - No - Phone Path

1. You take your Samsung Galaxy Note 7, open 20+ apps until smoke come out of it.

2. You throw your phone onto the create as the phone explodes.

3. As parts of the creature fall from the air, you run to the outside door.

4. You see your friends car. You enter it and speed away. WIN - CONGRATULATIONS
